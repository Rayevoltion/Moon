Shares & Dividends

You invest in a Share from a specific steward — a volume of meat, eggs, or produce you'll draw from all season. Each week, request dividends from your shares; we deliver to your door or a local Node, and pick up your food scraps on the way out.

Share

Your seasonal investment.

  • A specific volume from one steward — Mini, Quarter, Half, or Full.
  • Larger shares lock in a discounted per-pound price.
  • Stored for you; request what you want, when you want it.
Dividend

What you actually receive each week.

  • A smaller portion of your Share, requested Friday–Sunday.
  • Home delivery — or pickup at a Hub, Node, or Nook.
  • Food scrap collection included on delivery day.

A few honest questions.

What exactly is the community contribution?

A one-time payment when you join. It funds food hub growth, emergency funds for stewards, and scholarships for members who need them. All new members signing up by June 10 get 25% back as account credit.

What happens if I travel or my kitchen is full?

Skip a week. Your share is stored; request your dividend only when you want it.

What about my food scraps?

Picky Pig — our delivery partner — collects scraps on the same trip. They return to Yellow Barn to feed pigs and build compost.
